# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors of every background. Be respectful, specific and constructive. Respect privacy and differing experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation or exclude repeated/severe offenders. Apply proportionate measures, explain decisions privately where possible, and do not retaliate against good-faith reports.

Report non-sensitive conduct concerns to repository maintainers through a minimal moderation issue; do not post identifying details about affected people. A dedicated private conduct channel has not yet been established and must be confirmed before broad community launch. If no safe channel is available, do not disclose sensitive details publicly. Maintainers involved in a complaint should recuse themselves from its review.

These project-specific rules are MIT-licensed with the repository; they are not a claim of formal adoption of a third-party enforcement program.
